The expertise that makes the difference

Specialists in Mediterranean gardens.

Every project favours Mediterranean species that withstand heat and drought —olive trees, lavender, bougainvillea, rosemary, palms— for a garden that is both beautiful and easy to maintain.

For coastal settings, the design uses windbreak hedges and salt-tolerant plants such as oleander, tamarisk and date palms.

Knowing the Balearic climate is what makes the difference.
